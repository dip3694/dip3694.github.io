What the app does

Money Tracker is a personal income and expense tracker. You sign in with your Google account so the app can keep your records in a spreadsheet in your own Google Drive.

What access we ask for

The app asks only for the permission to see, create and edit the files that the app itself creates in your Google Drive (the "drive.file" permission). It uses this to create one spreadsheet called MONEY TRACKER DATA and to read and write your entries in it. The app cannot see your other Drive files.

Where your data is stored

Your entries, accounts, categories, budgets and auto-pay schedules are stored only in that spreadsheet in your Google Drive, and temporarily in your own phone or browser (for example, entries saved while you are offline). We run no server and keep no copy of your data. We cannot see it.

What we collect, share or sell

We do not collect, store, share or sell your personal information or your Google data. We do not use it for advertising.

Google API Services User Data Policy

Money Tracker's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Deleting your data

You can delete the MONEY TRACKER DATA spreadsheet from your Google Drive at any time. You can also remove the app's access at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Using "Sign out" inside the app removes the local copy from your device.

Contact

For questions, use the support email shown on the Google sign-in screen of this app.
